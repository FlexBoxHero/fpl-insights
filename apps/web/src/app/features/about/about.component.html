Methodology

FPL Insights is an experimental analytics tool. It is not affiliated with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

Data

  • Official FPL API (current season)
  • vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League (historical GW data)
  • football-data.co.uk (match results)

Models (Phase 1)

  • Teams: Poisson goal rates from rolling results + logistic clean-sheet calibration
  • Players: LightGBM on rolling FPL stats and fixture difficulty

Chip strategy (Phase 2)

  • Two of each chip per season: one in GW1–19 and one in GW20–38 (Wildcard, Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Free Hit). Only one chip can be active in a gameweek.
  • Timing uses remaining fixtures (blanks/doubles and difficulty swings) plus projected player points. Triple Captain also names the player to captain that week.
  • Advice is league-wide — it does not use your actual squad.

My Team (Phase 3)

  • Load your 15 via FPL team ID, the in-app picker, or a squad screenshot (screenshot needs a vision API key). Analysis uses your squad.
  • Rating compares your XI and captain to a template best XI from the same projections, then flags weak starters, hard fixtures, and one-transfer upgrades.
